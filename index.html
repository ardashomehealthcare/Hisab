Salary calculator (date range)

Salary is calculated for the date range you choose (From → To). The From date auto-fills: to the last salary paid to that employee, or to the joining date if the employee is a new hire with no salary paid yet. Leave days in the range are cut automatically. Monthly employees: per-day pro-rata (monthly wage ÷ days in that month × duty days), summed across any months in the range — a full month with no leave equals full salary. Per-day employees: rate × duty days. For 24-hr duty employees a time option appears so days are counted by 24-hour shifts (e.g. 8 AM → 8 AM = 1 day).

Client billing calculator Mid-month deal

If a client starts or discontinues service in the middle of a month, pick the client and the service period (From → To). For each calendar month in the range the bill is deal amount ÷ days in that month × service days. Whatever the client already paid in that same month is subtracted from that month's bill, so each month shows its own balance; the balances are then totalled. The deal amount is the client's agreed monthly package — set it once in Money Entry and it is remembered here.

Monthly profit / loss — 50-50 partner share

Calculated by calendar month: Client money received − Employee payments − Other expenses. Profit or loss is divided equally between both partners.

Connect your Google account

Every submit is saved on this device instantly, and also sent to your Google Sheet in its own tab (Employees, DutyLeave, EmployeePayments, ClientReceipts, Expenses) once you sign in. No Apps Script, no URLs — you sign in with your own Google account and the app talks to the Sheet directly.
Tip: paste it permanently into config.js so every device is pre-configured.

One-time setup (5 minutes, admin only)

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com → create (or pick) a project, e.g. Hisab.
  2. Menu APIs & Services → Library → search Google Sheets API → Enable.
  3. APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen → choose Internal (Google Workspace: only accounts in your organisation can sign in — this is the “Google Workspace OAuth” lock). If you don’t have Workspace, choose External and add your Google accounts as test users.
  4. APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type Web application.
  5. Under Authorised JavaScript origins add the exact address where the app is opened, e.g. https://ardashomehealthcare.github.io (add http://localhost:8000 too if you test locally). Sign-in only works from a listed origin.
  6. Copy the Client ID (ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it in config.js (HISAB_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID) — or in the box above → then press Sign in with Google and allow access to your Sheet.
  7. Done — every submit now goes straight from the browser to your Google Sheet. Each partner signs in with their own Google account (they need Editor access to the Sheet).
💡 The spreadsheet ID is locked into config.js (HISAB_SPREADSHEET_ID) — same sheet as before, so all existing data is kept.
