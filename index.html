Salary calculator (calendar month)

Salary is calculated from the duty days you have entered in the Duty/Leave tab (leave days are cut automatically). Rule: duty for the whole calendar month (30 or 31 days, 28/29 in Feb) = full month salary. Fewer days = per-day calculation (monthly wage ÷ days in that month × duty days). Per-day employees = rate × duty days.

Connect your Google Sheet

Every submit is saved on this device instantly, and also sent to your Google Sheet in its own tab (Employees, DutyLeave, EmployeePayments, ClientReceipts, Expenses) once connected.

One-time setup (5 minutes)

  1. Open sheets.google.com → create a new blank spreadsheet, name it e.g. Hisab.
  2. In the sheet menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any code there and paste the whole code from the file google-apps-script.gs (in this project). Press the 💾 save icon.
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment → gear icon ⚙ → choose Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  6. Google will ask permission — click Authorize, choose your account, click Advanced → Go to project (unsafe) → Allow. (It's your own script, this is safe.)
  7. Copy the Web app URL (ends with /exec) and paste it in the box above → Save & test connection.
  8. Press Send all saved data to Sheet once. Done — from now on every submit goes to the sheet automatically.
