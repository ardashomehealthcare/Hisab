Salary calculator (date range)

Salary is calculated for the date range you choose (From → To). The From date auto-fills: to the last salary paid to that employee, or to the joining date if the employee is a new hire with no salary paid yet. Leave days in the range are cut automatically. Monthly employees: per-day pro-rata (monthly wage ÷ days in that month × duty days), summed across any months in the range — a full month with no leave equals full salary. Per-day employees: rate × duty days. For 24-hr duty employees a time option appears so days are counted by 24-hour shifts (e.g. 8 AM → 8 AM = 1 day).

Connect your Google Sheet

Every submit is saved on this device instantly, and also sent to your Google Sheet in its own tab (Employees, DutyLeave, EmployeePayments, ClientReceipts, Expenses) once connected.

One-time setup (5 minutes)

  1. Open sheets.google.com → create a new blank spreadsheet, name it e.g. Hisab.
  2. In the sheet menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Press Ctrl+A (Mac: Cmd+A) to select everything in Code.gs, then Delete. Paste only the contents of google-apps-script.gs from this project (nothing after the last }). Press the 💾 save icon. If Apps Script says Unexpected token '*', leftover text is still in the file — select-all and paste again.
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment → gear icon ⚙ → choose Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  6. Google will ask permission — click Authorize, choose your account, click Advanced → Go to project (unsafe) → Allow. (It's your own script, this is safe.)
  7. Copy the Web app URL (ends with /exec) and paste it in the box above → Save & test connection.
  8. Press Send all saved data to Sheet once. Done — from now on every submit goes to the sheet automatically.
