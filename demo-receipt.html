Proposal

Status pill, itemised duty, big amount box, clean payment details, signature and a proper footer — still one page, WhatsApp-friendly.

What changed — and why

Numbers match the 1 pins inside the new receipt. Compare with Zoho / Razorpay / Vyapar receipts your clients already see every day.

1One brand name + contact header

Today header says “Ardas Caretaker Manager” but stamp says “ARDAAS HOME HEALTH CARE”. New design uses one legal name, with address, phone and email — like every professional invoice.

P0 • Must fix

2PAID / PARTIAL status pill

Green/amber pill at top-right is readable even in a small WhatsApp thumbnail. The big red stamp moves smaller, beside the signature, so it never covers amounts.

P0 • Must fix

3Itemised duty table

Service, period, duty days/hours and rate as separate columns instead of one grey paragraph. Client and employee can verify the calculation at a glance.

P1 • High value

4Big amount hero box

Total / Balance Due / Net Paid is the largest number on the page, colour-coded. This is the Razorpay/Zoho pattern — the eye lands there first.

P1 • High value

5Clean payment details

A simple strip shows only mode and date — Cash / UPI / Bank transfer with the payment date, kept clean and easy to read.

P1 • High value

6Signature lines

Authorised Signatory (client) and “Received by” employee signature make it a legal acknowledgment of the payment.

P1 • High value

7Screenshot becomes an attachment

Employee UPI screenshot is shown as a small labelled attachment strip, not a full-size photo dominating the receipt. Full image can stay on page 2.

P2 • Nice to have

8Professional footer

“Thank you”, query contact and “computer-generated” note replace the lone dashed “Thank you”. Builds trust and reduces support calls.

P2 • Nice to have

9Salary / Advance chip (employee)

Switch the toggle above: an Advance receipt looks different from Salary, with period, leave and net-paid summary payslip-style, like Pagarbook.

P1 • High value
