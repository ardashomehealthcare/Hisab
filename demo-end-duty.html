Interactive demonstration of the new "End Employee Duty" and "End Client Duty" buttons

📌 Scenario 1: End Employee Duty Only

Employee Rajesh is assigned to Client Mr. Sharma. After a few months, Rajesh leaves but Mr. Sharma needs another employee.

to

  • "End Client Duty" button still available (client may continue with another employee)
